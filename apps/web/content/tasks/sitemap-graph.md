The sitemap is sharded and the shards are generated from five catalogues, and
the only way to see the shape of that is to read the generator.

## What it touches

A rendered graph of shards and the pages under them, from the same index the
sitemap is built from.

## Done means

The graph and `sitemap.xml` are generated from one call, so they cannot
disagree about what exists.

## What it is not

Not an SEO audit. This draws what is published, and says nothing about whether
it should be.
