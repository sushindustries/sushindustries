Five tabs: index, get-started, guides, api, examples. A component missing one
renders four tabs and gives no sign that a fifth was intended.

`api` is the one that costs a reader most, because it is the section somebody
opens knowing exactly what they want - and the only one the doctor can
regenerate from the source when it drifts.
