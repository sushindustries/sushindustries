A component's own registry entry cannot answer this. It says what the
component needs; it says nothing about whether anything needs the component.
Both halves have to be absent for something to show up here - no parent in the
element graph, and no documentation page - because either one alone is a
reasonable state.

An empty answer is the good one. A name appearing here is either work that
deserves finishing or work that deserves deleting, and the entry itself will
not tell you which.
