Counted as it happens rather than projected from files, which makes an empty
answer mean something specific: nobody has visited, not that a sync has not
run. It is one of two tables in this database with that property.

The useful reading is the long tail rather than the top. A component page
nobody has ever opened, for a component that is documented and installable, is
a component whose problem is discovery rather than quality.
