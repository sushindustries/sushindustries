Section is a page section: a kicker, a heading and a body, wrapped in a
container and revealed top-down as the reader scrolls to it. Reach for it as
one of a page's top-level blocks whenever content should resolve as a
sequence instead of arriving as one block.

## Why it is built this way

The heading and the body reveal separately, offset by 80 milliseconds. That
gap is the whole trick - short enough that it does not read as a deliberate
sequence, long enough that the two halves do not land on the same frame and
arrive as one flat block instead.
